Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to NexusGrid. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our macOS application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

NexusGrid does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

All data generated by your use of the App (such as your inventory, items, custom items, and settings) is stored locally on your device (Mac) using Apple's standard local storage mechanisms (UserDefaults). We do not have servers, databases, or analytics trackers attached to this App. Your data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising frameworks that would collect data from your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information, our App is safe for children of all ages.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect contact information, we encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, contact us at our GitHub Issues page.
